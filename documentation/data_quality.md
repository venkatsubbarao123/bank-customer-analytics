## Treatment decisions

- The raw CSV is never modified.
- Dates and times are parsed with invalid values coerced to missing.
- DOBs after the transaction date or implying an age above 100 are treated as invalid and set to missing.
- Missing gender and location values are retained as `Unknown` rather than dropping transactions.
- Duplicate transaction IDs are removed only if they exist; repeated transactions by a customer are legitimate records.
- Required identifiers, dates, and amounts are required for the cleaned transaction table.
- Zero-value transactions are retained and reported because they are valid observations requiring business interpretation.

## Scope limitation

The transaction period is 2016-08-01 through 2016-10-21. August and September are complete calendar months; October is partial through October 21. A lower October total must not be interpreted as a confirmed decline in annual banking activity.
